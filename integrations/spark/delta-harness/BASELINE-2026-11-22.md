## Expectation updates (harness followed upstream on purpose)
- `ddl.rtas.replicationConflict` x2, `interact.flags.wapReplaceAtCreate` x2: #830 rejects RTAS+replication and
  RTAS+WAP at CREATE/ALTER time (400 "cannot enable RTAS ... together"), not at replace time.
- `interact.rtas.props.reservedPlane` x2 (G10), `hazard.rtas.wipesColumnTags` x2 (H3): #652 merges existing
  policies on RTAS; retention and PII tags now survive. Flipped to survival assertions.

## Harness adaptations (no production change)
- run-openhouse.sh: scala 2.12.18 jars from the Gradle cache when ~/.m2 is empty; `GRADLE_EXTRA_ARGS`; `HARNESS_JVM_ARGS`;
  writes META-INF/spring.factories for the stub remover. print-cp.init.gradle: `printHarnessCp` dependsOn the
  classpath so the uber jar/fixtures are built. Env.scala: `HtsStubSuppressor`/`HtsStubRemover` replace the
  dropped `@ConditionalOnProperty` on the fixture's `HouseTablesH2Repository`.
- Without `-Xmx` the full run was OOM-killed (exit 137, ~4.9 GB RSS) in this container.
