Tables can exist with no record, a record can exist with no tables. ## 3. What that design gives the Iceberg REST routes

### Route collision check [read, inferred]
- 870 mounts `/v1/{prefix}/namespaces` at the service root; `irc-integration` mounts `/iceberg/v1/...` with a `/iceberg/**` catch-all.
